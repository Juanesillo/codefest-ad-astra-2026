Counterspace Timeline, 1959 – 2022

This repository of counterspace weapons data is comprised of tests, technology demonstrations, and unusual behaviors first identified in our annual Space Threat Assessment report series.

Use the filter function to sort by weapons type, country, and date. Note that some entries have additional analysis, which describes the counterspace entry in more detail. You can easily find all those by filtering to show only entries with details.
